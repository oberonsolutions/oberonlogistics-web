About Us

Oberon Logistics, LLC is a proud Landstar Business Capacity Owner (BCO) specializing in Capacity Operations. Drawing on years of logistics expertise and a deep understanding of the trucking industry, we now operate our own professional fleet within the Landstar network.

Our team manages every aspect of capacity — from equipment readiness and compliance to real-time communication and load optimization — so Landstar Agents have dependable, high-performing trucks when they need them. We treat every load like it's our own because our success is tied directly to yours.

Headquartered in Sheridan, WY with a base of operations in Jefferson, GA, we are committed to the same standards of safety, reliability, and professionalism that define the Landstar network.
